# Contest App Architecture

This document records implementation-level architecture and behavior for
`apps/soar_score_contest`. It complements the route index in
`page-map.md`; it is not a replacement for the business and architecture
requirements under `./plan`.

Use this document when investigating contest-app navigation, page behavior,
shared widgets, services, reports, participants, workspace behavior, or sync.

The score domain lifecycle and its transactional invariants are documented in
`scoring-lifecycle.md`.

## Detailed Page and Component Notes

The notes below preserve the implementation guidance previously maintained in
the root `AGENTS.md`. Keep them current when the corresponding implementation
changes.

- Settings opens from the account control and keeps the current scope: `/contest-administration/settings` outside a contest, `/contest-administration/contests/:id/settings` (`contestSettingsPath`) inside one, so the contest rail stays.
- Account sync (`AccountSyncPage`) lists the account's own open conflicts and refused changes. Its pilot link reviews (`widgets/sync/pilot_link_review.dart`, in `ConflictResolveDialog`) are for disagreements that already exist: they show the GliderScore pilot number and the participant each device linked, and the director keeps the saved link, uses the other, or picks another participant (checked on the service, so a participant deleted meanwhile is refused). The service replaces a review when either link changes, so a decision on an old comparison is refused and the page loads the new one. One installation manages the remembered links of a director: the first to write one. The service refuses link writes from any other (`not_link_manager`, `PilotLinkCollisions.writerProblem`), including queued ones sent after reconnecting. A non-managing installation's imports show a notice with **Import without remembered links** (`PilotImportReviewList`, so both import entry points; nothing is saved until it is chosen, and `PilotImportController.apply` re-checks management; remembered matches there offer every participant so a wrong link can be corrected): `PilotImportLinkStore.linksManagedElsewhere` decides, every match is then confirmed by hand and `PilotImportStore.apply(rememberLinks: false)` leaves the saved links alone. Both pilot imports (`PilotImportController`, which the competition import also uses) hold back a pilot while `PilotImportLinkStore.pilotsUnderReview` names it. Server rules live in `soar_score_core` `PilotLinkCollisions`. Handing the links to a replacement computer is not built.
- "Configuration page" always means the contest-scoped page above, not the draft step inside the wizard.
- The contest-scoped "Reports" page lists the four reports (`widgets/reports/`, state in `services/reports/`): a context strip (lifecycle, scoring progress, result status with its help popover), then a card per report under Results and Documents to print. A report that cannot be produced shows a disabled Open with its reason and a link; warnings (preliminary scores, configuration drift, undrawn seeded rounds) sit on the card. Open launches the viewer through `ReportLauncher` (`services/reports/`): its own browser tab on the web (with a blocked-pop-up fallback, Open here), in place with Back to Reports on native. The viewer (`ReportViewerPage`) is a reusable frame: filters, options and page setup live in the URL query, the sheet is an unthemed paper page, and each report supplies a `ReportDefinition` (controls and pages). A report that reads data of its own overrides `dataKey` and `loadData` on `ReportSpec`; the viewer reads the data again only when the key changes. Overall Standings (`widgets/reports/standings/`, rules in `services/reports/standings/`) is built: it shows the stored standing as of the last round shown, read from `GET /contests/{id}/results?asOfRound=N`, and never recalculates. A report with data of its own extends `DataReportDefinition<D>` (`widgets/reports/viewer/report_definition.dart`): `loadData` answers a `ReportLoad<D>` (`ReportLoaded`, `ReportNoData` or `ReportNothingToLoad`) and `buildWith` receives a typed `D`, so a report never casts the viewer's data. Draw Matrix (printed title "Flight Matrix"; `widgets/reports/draw_matrix/`, rules in `services/reports/draw_matrix/`) is built: it reads the matrix once (`DrawMatrixData`, keyed by matrix revision) and applies the round range (`from`, `to`), Compact mode (`cmp`) and Additional pilot info (`inf`, `club`, `fai`, `nat`; off in compact mode, the choice kept) to it without another read; `DrawMatrixLayout` paginates it (a group is never split when it fits, a round header always sits with its first group, a continued round or group repeats its header); every column but the pilot's shrinks to its content and every cell is measured, so a wrapping cell makes its row taller in the plan instead of running off the page, and a crowded sheet sets `ReportDocument.notice`, a screen-only banner that names the columns to turn off (Team, Pilot number, Pilot class and the extra info are switchable). Pilot Scoring Cards (`widgets/reports/pilot_cards/`, rules in `services/reports/pilot_cards/`) is built: one landscape card per pilot, four or two to a Letter/A4 page, cut apart after printing. It reads the matrix, the scores and the invitations once (`PilotCardsData`, keyed by matrix revision and scores signature) and applies the round range (`from`, `to`), Include retired pilots (`ret`), the pilot choice (`pilots`: ids, `none` or `all`), Pre-fill (`pre`; off until a score exists, values that are not official print with an asterisk) Cards per page (`up`: `auto`, `four`, `two`) and the print margin (`mg`: `std`, a quarter inch, or `wide`, 10 mm) to it without another read. `CardSheetDecision` decides four-up or two-up from the rounds, not a fixed count (every card sized as if it had a QR code, so sending invitations later never changes the page count); `CardSizing` holds the millimetre rules (rows of 8 to 10 mm, QR 25 mm shrinking to a 22 mm floor, the footer text sits in the top block beside the QR code, and 8 rounds fit four to a page on Letter and A4 while 9 go two, 2 mm inset at cut lines). The columns follow the discipline (`CardColumns`). The report sets `fixedOrientation` (no Orientation control), `hasRunningFrame: false` and `barePageMargins(filters, facts)` (the print margin on every side, because printers cut the shared sheet's 4 mm sides and bottom) on `ReportSpec` because each card carries its own header and footer; it adds `ReportDocument.banners` and `beforePrint` (a reminder shown before the system print dialog) and `ReportSpec.changesOf` so the viewer redraws when an invitation is sent. A card shows a QR code (`qr` package, version 5 or larger for a longer link, error correction M, four modules of white on every side; a create that worked but could not be read back shows "QR codes were created" with Load QR codes, never a failed create) only when its pilot has an invitation; invitations are read and issued through `PilotInvitationSource` (`PilotInvitationSources.current`, default `ApiPilotInvitationSource`: `GET`/`POST /contests/{id}/invitations` on the hosted server, so `ReportDataRequest.publicationClient`, the same client publishing uses). Send invitations issues the links and then emails each pilot who has an address (`POST /contests/{id}/invitations/email`, one new request id per send, so a retry never mails twice); sending again for the same pilots re-emails them, and the confirmation says so. `PilotInvitationSession.lastEmail` keeps the per-pilot result (`InvitationEmailReport`); the links exist whatever happens to the email, and pilots whose email did not go out are listed by name in a result dialog that points to Copy link. Dialogs show names only, never addresses. A contest the hosted server does not have reads as not synchronized, no sign-in or a server that issues none as send unavailable with the reason beside the action, and a failed read leaves the cards printable with a retry banner (`InvitationPhase.loadFailed`). Invitations are also sent from the workspace (`InvitationsSection`, `WorkspaceSection.invitations`, directly under the setup checklist, and under the next-step card while Running) and from the Participants list (an Invitations menu beside Remove: Send invitations for the selected pilots, Copy link for exactly one pilot who has one). All three use `PilotInvitationSession` and the same confirmation (`sendInvitationsWithConfirmation`, `widgets/invitations/`); resend, regenerate and helper links are #791. `UnavailablePilotInvitationSource` leaves cards without a code for tests; the lifecycle journey (phase 4) swaps in `StubPilotInvitationSource` (`integration_test/support/`) because its embedded server has no invitations. Scoring Sheet (`widgets/reports/scoring_sheet/`, rules in `services/reports/scoring_sheet/`, #479) is built: the draw by round and group with blank, ruled cells for officials to write scores in by hand (9 mm rows, real 5 mm tick boxes, black rules only). It reads the matrix and scores once (`ScoringSheetData`, keyed by matrix revision and scores signature) and applies the round range (`from`, `to`), Club (`club`, off), Team, Pilot number and Pilot class (`team`, `num`, `cls`, each only when the contest uses it), Validated (`val`, on), Include retired pilots (`ret`) and Pre-fill (`pre`) without another read. The columns come from the discipline (`ScoringSheetColumns`, reusing the `CardColumns` height and restart rules): Lane when the draw has lanes, the pilot, the optional identity columns, then the score components, Penalty, Reflight and Validated. Writing cells get spare width first, then the pilot's name up to 70 mm. `ScoringSheetLayout` never splits a group that fits a page, keeps a round heading with its first group and repeats a carried-on round or group as "(Continued)". The report picks the orientation itself until the director chooses one (`ReportSpec.suggestedOrientation`, which the other reports leave at their default): Portrait when every column fits across it at its narrowest, Landscape otherwise. Columns that still do not fit are drawn at their narrowest and continue on further pages, whole columns only, with the same rows, column headings and round and group headings on each page of a band so pages taped side by side line up (nothing repeats the pilot). A retired pilot (`retiredAtRound` is the first round they no longer fly) is left out of the rounds from then on; with Include retired pilots the name carries "(R)". Pre-fill prints entered scores (unofficial ones with an asterisk, shaded and in bold monospace so a printer that drops grey loses nothing) and is off until a score exists. A legend line under the table (only when a mark needs it: "(R) = retired", "Includes scores through round N · \* not official") sits above the frame's standard footer; the sheet uses the standard header and footer. Print and Save as PDF go through `ReportPrinter` (`services/reports/viewer/`); the app uses `PlatformReportPrinter`, held in `ReportPrinters.current` so an E2E journey can swap the two system dialogs for recorders. Engine (decided 2026-10-21, #476): raster. `RasterReportPdfBuilder` (behind the `ReportPdfBuilder` interface) paints each finished sheet page in an off-screen render tree and places it in the PDF as an image, so the printout matches the sheet exactly and each report has one layout; PDF text is not selectable or searchable. If testers ask for that, add a vector `ReportPdfBuilder` for one report at a time (start with the standings table) and keep the raster one as the fallback. The Page group has "Pages to print" (`pp` in the URL, parsed by `PrintPageRange`): blank for every page, or `1` or `2-4`; `printing` cannot pick pages of a sheet the app draws, so the viewer sends only those pages, and Print and Save as PDF are off while the text is not a range of the sheet. On Windows the print dialog is the classic Print Setup one, not the newer `windowsModernDialog` one, which ignores the paper size and orientation the app asks for and starts from the printer's defaults; the classic dialog's Properties button reaches the printer's own double-sided and colour options, which the app cannot set itself. Print uses `printing`'s `layoutPdf` (the web reports nothing after its dialog, so the viewer stays quiet); Save as PDF downloads in the browser (`sharePdf`) and uses a save dialog (`file_selector`) on Windows, because `printing` cannot share on Windows. The Windows build of `printing` downloads a pdfium binary at build time; on the web it loads pdf.js from a CDN only for its own PDF rasteriser, which this app does not use.
- The Scoring route takes optional `?round=N&group=G` (built by `contestScoringPath`); the next-step card and the progress card's tiles use it. The page is official F5J score entry for one group (#123): round and group navigation, a row per pilot saved on its own, Score check, and the save, conflict and not-permitted states. Its state lives in `services/scoring/` (`ScoringController`); a row with a waiting pilot entry offers Mark official (`ScoringController.markOfficial`, `POST .../assignments/:id/scores/official`) and the toolbar offers Mark all valid rows official (`markAllOfficial`, the group route, one outcome per row; a row with typed edits, an open review or a lock is left out and a 503 means try again); on a native installation whose contest syncs (and whose first upload finished) both calls are answered `202`/`queued` and only record a `promote_score` intent in the outbox, which the service decides on the next sync (`PromotionIntentPolicy`, `NativePromotionIntents`; the slot refuses other saves with `promotion_pending` meanwhile); a score the contest accepted automatically (official, origin automatic, review not yet closed: `ScoringRow.isAutomaticUnconfirmed`) shows "Official · automatic" with a Confirm button (`ScoringController.confirmAutomatic`, the same promote route naming the official attempt and revision; no second version, review closes), and the history names "System (automatic)"; the lock action is not built yet. The Indicators column says where a score came from and stays on an official row: Pilot entry shows when a pilot or helper first entered the values (`ScoringRow.isPilotEntry`, from the waiting attempt or, once official, `ScoreSlot.officialAttemptAuthority`, which the service reads from the attempt the official score adopted; a director's own entry, typed over a submission or not, has no chip) and a Review chip is built but hidden until review decisions exist (#145). The status cell adds at most one line of its own beyond the sync and lock notes: "Fix to save" in red on a row the director left with a mistake that blocks saving; Mark official and Confirm are quiet text buttons whose reason for being off is their tooltip only. Unsaved Raw and Group scores are muted italic with a tooltip, and a partly entered row says "Incomplete" under Raw score. The row menu opens the score history (`GET .../assignments/:id/scores/history`) and retires or reinstates the pilot from the round in view (a participation update, which recalculates the results); the group menu opens the group's history (#432). The grid has three layouts picked by the page's width (`ScoreGridMode`, `widgets/scoring/score_grid_columns.dart`): wide (1140 px and up; one line per pilot under the column heading), medium (700 px up to that, the iPad, the smallest size the app supports; two lines per pilot, `ScoreGridColumns.medium*`: pilot, time, height, landing and the row menu on the first, under the same shortened heading, and Raw score, Group score, Penalties, Indicators and the status on the second, each with its own label) and compact (a stacked card, phones, not a supported size).
- The account control sits at the bottom of the rail (`widgets/shell_account_button.dart`): the user's picture with a menu (name, Settings, Sign out) when signed in, a gear otherwise (offline: menu with Sign in and Settings). Settings opens the Settings page (`/contest-administration/settings`, system scope, no rail entry): the theme choice (System, Light, Dark; `ThemeController` in `services/theme/`, kept per account in the `LocalUiStore`) and the text size (Default, Large, Extra large; `TextSizeController` in `services/text_size/`, kept the same way). `app.dart` applies the size in the `MaterialApp.router` builder through `PreferredTextScaler`, which multiplies the device's text scale by 1, 1.25 or 1.5 and caps the product at 2.5, so layouts must wrap, stack or scroll instead of assuming a text height; `test/server/text_size_layout_test.dart` sweeps the key pages at every size and fails on any overflow (add a new page to its route list). It also holds the account's preferred time zone and locale (`PreferencesController` in `services/preferences/`, `preferencesOf(context)`; saved to the account through `AppBootstrap.savePreferences`) and "Reset panels to defaults" for the remembered hidden and minimized panels (`CollapsibleAreasController` in `services/collapsible_areas/`, one stored choice map per account, shared by all contests; register each new collapsible panel in `collapsibleAreas` and read it with `collapsibleAreasOf(context)`). Times shown to the director (last saved, last synced, history) go through `DirectorTime.of(context)` so they follow the preferred zone and locale; times pilots see keep using `ContestTime` with the contest's zone. User-visible text goes in `lib/l10n/app_en.arb`. `UiAccount` (`services/ui_account/`, `uiAccountOf(context)`) names the account whose UI-only state to read and write; use it as the account id for every `LocalUiStore` call.
- The participant editor is one page with three section cards (`widgets/participant_editor/`), its state and API calls in `ParticipantEditorController` (`services/participants/`). Validation shows per field once left and as a summary after a save attempt; a matching email is a decision panel (Use existing / Change the email; two participants cannot share an email), not an error; a stale revision offers Reload or a field-by-field review; Save is off in edit until something changed; add mode has Save and add another, which keeps club and location. The storage chip comes from `AppSync` (native only). `remoteMatchingUnavailable` and `managedFields` on the page are built but not wired until #247.
- Rail and header code: `widgets/shell_rail.dart` (destinations and create button), `shell_header.dart` (breadcrumbs), `shell_status_panel.dart`, `shell_stub_page.dart`.
- The contest Participants page (`pages/contest/participants_page.dart`, state in `services/contest_participants/`, `ParticipantsController`) lists the stored people with the wizard's list widgets (`widgets/participants/`). Role, class and team edits wait behind the Save bar (`participants_save_bar.dart`) and save row by row (`PUT .../participations/{id}` for role and class with the revision the row was read at, team memberships for the team); adding (search or the participant editor, with a role choice and the late-add warning after the draw) and removing act at once. A pilot has flights when any score slot of theirs is past not entered (read from `GET .../scores`); that blocks Remove and a non-flying role (`has_scores` from the server). A drawn pilot with no scores can still be removed or made non-flying: the server removes their matrix rows and empty score slots with a tombstone each and leaves the matrix revision alone (`ParticipationsService`, `MatrixAssignmentRepository.removeForParticipation`; the answer carries `matrix_rows_removed`), the confirmations say so, and `banner-redo-draw` leads to the Matrix page's redraw. How a row is drawn beyond its values (edited cells, state, note, locks) comes from `ParticipantRowStyle` (`participants_row_styles.dart`). Class is required for scoring pilots when classes are on. A pilot who joins a drawn contest (added, or changed from a non-flying role) is put in the last group that can take them in every round through `POST .../matrix/late-placements` (`LatePlacementService`: a normal late add per round, going back to earlier groups when one is full, locked, flown or holds a teammate; a round already fully scored gets them in its last group with a blank, zero official score; a round no group can take them in is reported and left to the Matrix page, which only older data reaches: the server refuses the add or the change to a pilot role with 409 `no_room_in_round` when a round that is not fully scored has no group with room, since a group holds `ceil(originals / groups)` pilots and unseated pilots take open seats first, `LateAddCapacity`; the page keeps the add failure banner with the server's text). With no scores yet the page first offers to redraw the matrix with them; declining places them anyway. Completed contests are read-only. Each pilot's models are edited in a window (`widgets/participants/pilot_models_dialog.dart`, backends in `services/participants/pilot_models_backend.dart`) that saves as it goes (`/contests/{id}/model-links`); pending-deletion is not built.
- The wizard's Participants step (step 4) lives in `widgets/new_contest/participants/`; its table, cards, cells, team combobox, bulk bar and search field are shared (`widgets/participants/`, over the `ParticipantRow` and `ParticipantListRules` interfaces in `services/participants/`) so the contest Participants page can reuse them. Email shows as a status (Has email, None, Private), never as an address. The Models column opens the same window over the draft; the models are sent when the contest is created (a typed one is also added to the person's profile) and a pilot without one only shows a notice.
- The workspace page is a frame of section and control slots (`widgets/workspace/`) arranged by `WorkspaceLayoutPlan` (`services/workspace/`). It has no page-card list; links sit next to the fact they act on. The controls column starts with the status summary (labelled lifecycle, scoring, administration, publication, visibility, results hiding and local archive values) and holds the lifecycle, scoring and administration cards (`widgets/workspace/controls/`, rules in `services/workspace/controls/`); lock and complete ask first, and the archive card is disabled because the API has no archive action. The next-step card and the control cards share `ContestActionRunner` and the snackbar texts in `contest_transition.dart`.
- The workspace's Recent activity card (`widgets/workspace/activity/`, rules in `services/workspace/activity/`) lists the newest audit events (state transitions and successful publication events; the actor shows as "you" only for the acting account) read once with the workspace facts (`ActivityFacts`); a failed read shows a retry in the card only. View all is disabled until the audit page exists. Configuration, matrix and score-correction events are not written yet.
- The "Sync" rail entry and the sync item in the status panel appear only in builds that synchronize (native). Screens reach sync through `AppSync` (`services/sync/`), never the sync package. A refused change or open conflict adds a row to the workspace needs-attention list that links to the sync page. The sync page also lists the open conflicts and decides each in a dialog (`widgets/sync/conflict_resolve_dialog.dart`).
- Opening a contest from the list goes through `ContestOpenAction` / `contestOpenPath`, which picks the page by contest state.
