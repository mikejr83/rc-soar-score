# Data Model Reconciliation Findings

Reconciliation of `plan/data-model` against `plan/architecture-requirements` (including `data/`), `plan/business-requirements`, and the decisions recorded in `plan/tickets/pre-implementation`. Reviewed on 2026-10-02 against branch `claude/data-model-entity-reconcile-6f5839`.

Rule used: architecture requirements win over the data model, and business requirements win over both. Where the model is deliberately ahead of the requirements (mostly the account work), the finding says "update the requirement" instead of "change the model".

## Summary

- 41 entity documents, 41 entities in the ERD plus `CONTEST_CONFIGURATION_PRESET`, which has no document (presets are a deliberate addition per the design handoff, but no requirement covers them).
- Most entities are close to the requirements. The matrix, round, assignment, placeholder, and penalty documents are the strongest and need only small fixes.
- Seven items block writing models and repositories without rework: G-01 to G-07 below. Each either contradicts a requirement or leaves a required concept with nowhere to live.
- Fourteen decisions need you (section 5). Each has a recommendation.

## Owner decisions from the design handoff

## 3. Blocking findings

### G-01 Invitation contradicts the access model (resolved: `invitation.md` rewritten)

`invitation.md` says an invitation is single-use and moves to `Used`. `self-scoring-invites.md` (6 to 8) and `contest-self-scoring.md` require the link to keep working so the participant can return to the overview. Only the latest non-revoked revision authorizes access.

- States should be `Active`, `Replaced`, `Revoked`, `Expired`. Track `first_used_at` and `last_used_at`, not a terminal `Used`.
- The "one pilot invitation, many crew invitations per pilot" line contradicts "latest revision per participation link". A non-flier's invitation targets the non-flier's own participation.
- Missing: `expires_at` (QR tokens have none, magic URLs expire 24 hours after completion), `issued_by`, `replaces_invitation_id`, and an idempotency key (`self-scoring-invites.md`, delivery item 4).
- `rate_limit_attempts` is a service concern, not data. Remove it.
- Delivery outcomes as a JSON array is acceptable, but the architecture says issuance and delivery are separate and auditable. Consider an `InvitationDelivery` child.
- `account.md` says `Invitation.redeemed_by_account_id` exists. It does not.
- Stale text: `contestId`, `linkId_part`, and a source path (`participating-contestants-and-teams/contest.md`) that does not exist.

### G-02 Lock model is narrower than the requirements

Architecture and business both require locks at score, group, round, and contest level (`score-review-and-validity-state.md`, `participant-score-review.md`, `contest-official-scoring.md`). `score_placeholder.md` states group and assignment locks "are not modeled", `Round.status` covers only rounds, and there is no Group entity even though `matrix-and-assignments.md` says a group belongs to a round. See decision D1.

### G-03 Calculation output has no coherent home

- The architecture requires derived values with exact input revisions: configuration revision, matrix revision, rules version, and calculation version. `ScoreCalculationResult` has the configuration revision only, no matrix revision, and no rules version.
- Two grains share one table. Per-flight values (raw flight score, group score) sit beside per-participation values (contest total, dropped decision, rank, team total). The penalty total and ranking are explicitly "stored with the participation standing" (`contest_penalty.md`), which this table cannot express.
- There is no "calculation revision" or staleness marker, although `discipline-scoring.md` requires affected derived values to be marked stale and recomputed into a new revision.
- The stage enum omits the penalty stage that sits between dropped rounds and the final total.
- Ties need a shared placement (`T-1`) and the tie-break inputs. Nothing holds them.
- See decision D3.

### G-04 Lane is required, but lane assignment is optional

`business-requirements/contest-configuration.md` says lanes default to `None` and are assigned only when the director picks `Random`. `MatrixAssignment.lane` is required, `Round` derives lane count from group size, and `draw_config` has no lane mode. The old `RoundConfig.lane_mode` (`Shared`/`Separate`) never matched the business values either. Make `lane` nullable, add `lane_mode` (`None`/`Random`) to `draw_config`, and relax the lane uniqueness and range invariants when it is null.

### G-05 ReFlight does not model the business process

- Lifecycle: business has `Proposed`, `DrawPending`, `AwaitingResponses`, `ReadyToFly`, `Flown`, `Scored`, `Finalized`, `Cancelled`. The entity has `Open`, `Awarded`, `Finalized`.
- Types are missing: move to group, random draw, re-fly entire group. `type` is a free string.
- The sequence is per source group ("re-flight number"), and a re-flight of a re-flight links to the preceding attempt. The entity keys sequence on contest plus credited round, and has no source group or source assignment and no previous-re-flight link.
- Random draw needs a seed, the requested and achievable group size, and a flag for the director's confirmed exception. Awarding outside the discipline's preferred method needs a deviation flag.
- The five UUID arrays and two JSON arrays should be rows: a `ReFlightParticipant` child with role (awarded, candidate, excluded, selected), response (pending, accepted, declined), who responded (participant or director override), timestamps, and notification outcome.
- `credited_round` is an int, not an FK to `Round`. `finalizing_actor_id` is required but cannot exist until finalization. `discipline_version` and `rules_version` duplicate each other.
- The "extra group flown after all other groups" re-flight cannot be represented: `MatrixAssignment.group` must be at most `Round.group_count`.
- Cancellation is allowed before finalization and must keep history. There is no cancelled state or timestamp.

### G-06 Participation: status, retirement, and role

- Retirement is specified as an overlay on the participation link: `status = Retired`, `retired_at_round = N`. The architecture says the engine treats unflown rounds from N as zero without creating records. `participation_status` is `Pending/Accepted/Active/Completed/Withdrawn`, an invitation-style flow that no requirement describes (directors add participants directly). There is no `Retired`, no `retired_at_round`, and no actor (participants self-retire, directors may too).
- `score_placeholder.md` says retired outcomes are recorded as official score components. That conflicts with the overlay. Reconcile it with ticket 012 (`NOT_ENTERED`, `ZERO`, `RETIRED` as OutcomeStatus, retirement creating no records).
- `role_id` is missing from the field table while the invariants reference it, along with `cls` and `team_membership_id` (stale names). `TeamMembership` already points at the participation, so `team_membership_id` would be circular. The ERD has `role_type` but no `role_id`.
- `classification` is required in the entity, nullable in the ERD, and optional in the business requirements (only when classes are enabled, pilots only). `role_type` is the same.
- `scoring_uuid` is typed UUIDv7. `self-scoring-invites.md` requires a non-sequential identifier. v7 is time-ordered. Use v4 for this one field.
- No `contest_id` unique constraint with `participant_profile_id` is written, though "once per contest" is stated.

### G-07 Required `Cumulative Penalty` and core contest options have no definition

- Every discipline must expose an integer `Cumulative Penalty` raw component (`discipline-scoring.md`, `participant-score-entry.md` 6 to 7). It appears nowhere in the data model or the discipline examples, and the handoff removed it from `ScoreAttempt` in favour of flight and administrator-only contest penalties. Either update the three requirement documents or restore the component. The service must be able to reject a payload that omits it, so it needs a defined key and place: seed it as a locked core row in every profile version's score fields, or inject it in code and document the key.
- `ContestConfigurationRevision.config_json` has no defined shape. The requirements list many contest-level options that are not discipline-specific: group-score mode, rounding or truncation and decimals, dropped-round policy, review policy, publication update policy, teams, classes, roles, models, participant numbers, team pilot count, tie-break option, team protection, draw mode. `DisciplineConfigField` covers discipline-specific settings only. Discipline defaults for core options (ALES defaults group score to `Time`, Thermal to `Normalized`, F3J truncates, F5J rounds) have no home.
- Recommended: a `plan/data-model/contest-options.md` that lists the core option keys and types, with `config_json` split into `core` and `discipline` namespaces, and with per-discipline core defaults as config-field rows or a `default_contest_options` column on `DisciplineProfile`. This is consistent with ticket 004 (effective config = profile plus per-field overrides).

## 4. Other findings

### Missing concepts the requirements need

- **Installation.** Each contest has one active administration installation, with identity, last-sync state, activation, and revocation (`offline-synchronization.md`). `MutationRecord.source_installation` is a bare UUID, `SyncCursor` invariants say "per installation" but the entity has no installation column, and the ERD has none. Needs an `Installation` entity and an active-installation pointer on the contest.
- **Sync stream.** The architecture defines a per-contest monotonic `contest_mutation_seq`. `MutationRecord` has no sequence column, so there is nothing to put a cursor against.
- **Delist and deletion request.** `contest.md` has `deletion_status` only. Requester, decision, administrator, and the prior publication and visibility to restore on denial have no home. `account.md` also raises a delist request per publication.
- **Silent review record.** A participant submission that loses to an offline official edit is kept as a silent review record (`offline-synchronization.md`). `ReviewRequest` has no origin, so silent records cannot be told from real requests.
- **Participant sharing consent and email visibility.** The architecture says ownership and sharing consent are recorded separately, and email needs an explicit visibility policy. `ParticipantProfile` has neither, and `ParticipantAccountLink` has no consent or candidate-versus-confirmed status.
- **Profile alias.** Ticket 011 decided a local profile is aliased or merged into the shared profile on a tiered-key match. There is no `merged_into_profile_id` or alias entity.
- **Publication attempts.** Failed and rejected publishes must be retained and publish requests must be idempotent. `PublicationProjection.result = Failed` forces a full immutable projection row for a failure, and there is no request id. Use a `PublicationAttempt` with a request id, and keep projections for successes only. The anonymous-publisher email verification (10 minute code, 2 minute resend) has no home either, unless it is deliberately service-only state.
- **Owner scope for director data.** Sync is scoped to the director's data. Profiles, roles, and model catalog rows have no `owner_account_id`, so they cannot be scoped or synced. `LandingTable` already has one. Roles are described as director-owned or system defaults, and models as director-owned or shared.
- **Group.** See G-02.
- **Contest metadata.** `contest.md` requires creation and last-modified metadata. The entity has none. Also missing: a field for the display-only "total rounds intended" in seeded draws, and a provenance link for contests created from a previous contest.

### Entities that are wrong against the requirements

- **Role.** `permission` (`Admin`/`Score`/`Read`) appears in no requirement, and "Pilot roles are non-flier helpers" in the invariants is wrong. The requirements define a role by type only (`Pilot` or `NonFlier`), exactly one `Pilot` role, director-defined non-flier roles, and defaults Pilot, Helper, Team Manager. Roles are not contest-scoped. Contest data keeps the role id and a snapshot, so roles need soft-delete (`archived_at`) or the participation must snapshot the name.
- **TeamMembership.** `role_id` (Captain/Pilot/Helper) duplicates the role on the participation, and Captain is not a role in any requirement. No `contest_id`.
- **Team.** `pilot_totals` is per team, but the business requirement and example make it one contest-level number ("three pilot scores for the team score"). A team smaller than the number needs a rule. See D11.
- **ParticipantProfile.** `is_shared=false` is documented as "local to a single contest". The requirements say director-owned profiles are reusable across that director's contests (frequent participants), and `is_shared` means a participant account opted into the shared directory. Email: the business requirements make it required, the architecture makes it optional. See D12.
- **ParticipantAccountLink.** Says links are soft-deleted and never removed, but `account.md` step 4 deletes them. No status or end date to support "soft".
- **LandingResult and LandingTable.** `LandingResult` has `point_value` described as the entered value, and no column for the resolved points, which is the whole purpose of the table. The F5J example confirms it: `point_value` 0 pairs with "Within 10 m - 20 pts". `LandingTable` invariants cite `revision` and a lifecycle, but there is no `revision` or `status` column, and the ERD has neither. `input_unit` allows meters or feet only, though business landing bonuses can be keyed by points. A score must retain the key, the resolved points, and the table revision (`discipline-scoring.md`), and `ContestConfigurationRevision` has no landing table reference in the entity (the ERD has one). See D4.
- **DisciplineScoreField.** `options` is allowed only for `enum`, but `enum` is not in its type list. `zero_reason_code` points to a `DisciplineZeroReason` entity that no longer exists (merged into `DisciplineRule.rule_key`). `landing` fields have no way to name which landing table to use except a UUID tucked into a config field's `default_value`. The architecture also wants unit, precision, and field kind (input, status flag, derived) on every field definition.
- **DisciplineProfile.** `created_by` and `superseded_by` are strings. They should be an Account FK and a profile id. "Keyed by `discipline_profile_id` and `version`" in the purpose line is stale (the version is on the profile row).
- **ContestConfigurationRevision.** `profile_version` duplicates `DisciplineProfile.version` once each version is its own row, so it can drift. The ERD has `revision_number`, `landing_table_id`, `source_preset_id`, and `jsonb`. The entity has `current` and different names. "Exactly one current" is better as a pointer on `Contest`.
- **ScoreAttempt.** Missing server acceptance time and calculation version, both listed under "stored scoring data". It says review proposals are attempts, while `ReviewRequest` stores them as `proposed_components` and `OfficialScore.score_attempt_id` is required. Approving a review therefore has no attempt to cite. See D9.
- **ReviewRequest.** Stale parent (`Score`) and field name (`placeholder_id`, ERD says `score_placeholder_id`). No `contest_id` (the sync-scoping rule needs it) and no `revision`. "One open request per assignment" has no unique-index note.
- **MatrixAssignment.** "A scored assignment cannot be moved" conflicts with `move-pilot.md`. See D2.
- **PublicationIdentity and AccountPublicProfile.** `publication_identity.md` references `Account` directly, `account_public_profile.md` says publications never do, and refers to a `PublicationParticipant` entity that does not exist. The ERD says `owner text`. `account_id` is required, but anonymous publication has no account. No delisted state, and no stored prior visibility for restore.
- **PublicationProjection.** The entity and ERD disagree completely (`source_revision`/`content` versus a dozen named fields). Discipline is required in the projection by `contest-publishing.md` and is missing. No `contest_id`.
- **Sync and audit.** `actor_id` and `resolved_by` are `FK(User.userId)` and there is no `User` entity (use Account, including guest accounts). `entity_id` is `text` in `MutationRecord` and `ConflictRecord` but UUID elsewhere. `AuditEvent` requires "server timestamp >= client timestamp", which breaks with client clock skew (client time is audit-only per the architecture). `Tombstone.deleted_at` is "client-local time", against the all-UTC rule. `AuditEvent.action` lacks invitation revoked or replaced, delivery request, failed access, rejected mutation, and deletion request. `Contest` says one `SyncCursor`, but there is one per installation.
- **Contest.** Start and end are "in the contest's timezone", against all-UTC storage. Store UTC plus `timezone`.

### Model concepts with no requirement behind them

- `AccountPublicProfile`, `AccountIdentity` (fine as design), account `kind` (`guest`, `local`, `deleted`), `archived`, claiming, and the deletion workflow. Neither `accounts.md` (architecture) nor the business `accounts.md` mentions them. The business rule is that participants and anonymous directors need no account. Update the requirements (D8).
- `CONTEST_CONFIGURATION_PRESET` (ERD only, no document, `source_preset_id`). No requirement mentions presets (D13).
- `Role.permission`, `Invitation.rate_limit_attempts`, `TeamMembership` "Captain" (see above).
