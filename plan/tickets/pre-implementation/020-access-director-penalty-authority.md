# Ticket 020 — Access: Director Penalty and Disqualification Authority

- **Category:** Access / Permissions
- **Priority:** Low
- **Status:** Open

## Context

The authority to issue a director penalty or disqualification is not explicitly defined.

## Questions

1. Can only the contest director issue a penalty or disqualification, or can a delegate director also do so?
2. Is there an approval workflow (e.g., a second official must confirm), or is it unilateral?
3. Can a penalty be issued after a score is confirmed by the pilot?
4. How is a penalty surfaced to the pilot — is there a notification, or is it only visible in the score breakdown?

## Decisions Needed

- Who can issue penalties/disqualifications.
- Whether an approval workflow is required.
